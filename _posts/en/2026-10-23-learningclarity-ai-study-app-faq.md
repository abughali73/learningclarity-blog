faq:
  - group: "Getting started"
    q: "What is LearningClarity?"
    a: "LearningClarity is an AI-powered study platform for the web, iPhone and Android. It turns any topic you describe, or your own notes and PDFs, into step-by-step lessons, quizzes, timed exams, podcasts and spoken tutoring, and can end with a certificate anyone can verify."
  - group: "Getting started"
    q: "Who is LearningClarity for?"
    a: "Individual learners, students preparing for exams, parents helping a child, teachers, and teams training staff. Explanations and questions are pitched at the learner's age, which is taken from the date of birth given at sign-up."
  - group: "Getting started"
    q: "How do I create an account?"
    a: "Sign up on the web, the iPhone app or the Android app with your Google, Microsoft or Apple account, or with an email and password. During onboarding you add your date of birth and choose your language. [Quick start guide](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/quick-start/)."
  - group: "Getting started"
    q: "Is LearningClarity free to try?"
    a: "Yes. Every new account starts with 300 free credits, no card is asked for, and there is no trial that starts charging. You only buy credits if and when you need more."
  - group: "Getting started"
    q: "What's the quickest way to start learning?"
    a: "Choose **Describe Topic**, type what you want to learn, and LearningClarity builds a roadmap of subtopics in seconds. Then open the first subtopic in Explain Mode. Describing a topic is also the cheapest way to start."
  - group: "Getting started"
    q: "Which mode should I use first?"
    a: "Explain, Quiz and Exam do different jobs. Explain Mode builds understanding, Quiz Mode checks it with low pressure, and Exam Mode measures it formally. Most people go Explain, then Quiz, then Exam for each subtopic, but you can enter any mode directly. [Which mode, and where to start](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/modes-and-start/)."
  - group: "Getting started"
    q: "How is LearningClarity different from a general AI chatbot?"
    a: "A chatbot answers one question at a time. LearningClarity turns a subject into a structured roadmap, teaches it in short sections with knowledge checks, quizzes and examines you on it, tracks your progress and mastery, and can award a verifiable certificate. [Choosing an AI study tool](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/choosing-a-tool/)."
  - group: "Creating topics and uploading material"
    q: "What's the difference between describing a topic and uploading material?"
    a: "**Describe Topic** builds a topic from a sentence or a few paragraphs (up to 5,000 characters) about what you want to learn. **Upload Resources** builds it from your own files, so explanations, quizzes and exams follow exactly what your course or employer requires."
  - group: "Creating topics and uploading material"
    q: "How do I write a good topic description?"
    a: "Be specific and add your goal or level. \"Supervised learning algorithms for classification, for a data analyst exam\" gives a sharper roadmap than \"Machine learning\". [Creating your first topic](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/create-topic/)."
  - group: "Creating topics and uploading material"
    q: "What files can I upload?"
    a: "PDF files up to 40 MB each, images in PNG, JPG or JPEG up to 10 MB each (at most 7 per upload) and plain text .txt files up to 5 MB, with up to 50 MB in one upload. Several files in one upload are read together as one body of material. [Studying from your own notes](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/own-notes/)."
  - group: "Creating topics and uploading material"
    q: "Can I upload Word documents or PowerPoint slides?"
    a: "Not directly. Export them to PDF first and they upload normally."
  - group: "Creating topics and uploading material"
    q: "Can I upload videos, audio recordings or YouTube links?"
    a: "No. Audio and video files, including lecture recordings, and YouTube links aren't supported as sources. Use the slides, notes or a transcript as a PDF or text file instead."
  - group: "Creating topics and uploading material"
    q: "Why did my PDF fail to upload?"
    a: "The most common causes are a scanned PDF without a text layer (if you can't select the text, the app can't read it), a password-protected PDF, or a file over the size limit. Unlock the file, use a text-based version, or split large material into smaller topics."
  - group: "Creating topics and uploading material"
    q: "Can I study code, maths or technical documentation?"
    a: "Yes. Technical topics get worked examples, diagrams and callouts, and maths problem sets can be uploaded as PDFs or images so the questions follow your problem type. Export documentation sites to PDF; repositories and website links can't be read directly. [Code and technical material](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/technical-material/)."
  - group: "Creating topics and uploading material"
    q: "Can I choose which subtopics to focus on?"
    a: "Yes. After the roadmap is created you pick the subtopics you want to learn, and completion is tracked for each one."
  - group: "Explain, Quiz and Exam modes"
    q: "What is Explain Mode?"
    a: "Explain Mode is an interactive AI tutor. It teaches a subtopic one short section at a time, and you select Next when you're ready to continue. Sections can include charts, callouts and worked examples. [Explain Mode](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/explain-mode/)."
  - group: "Explain, Quiz and Exam modes"
    q: "What do Simplify and Explain Further do?"
    a: "**Simplify** rephrases the current section in simpler words. **Explain Further** adds detail, examples and analogies. Neither moves you forward, so you never lose your place. You can also ask your own question about a section or ask for an example."
  - group: "Explain, Quiz and Exam modes"
    q: "What is Visualize?"
    a: "In Explain Mode, **Visualize** generates a clean, labelled diagram of the current concept, with labels in the topic's language. [Visual learning with AI diagrams](/en/2026/10/visual-learning-with-ai-diagrams/)."
  - group: "Explain, Quiz and Exam modes"
    q: "What are Knowledge Checks?"
    a: "Short multiple-choice questions inside Explain Mode that make sure you understood a section before moving on. You get feedback on every answer, and **Retry** lets you try again."
  - group: "Explain, Quiz and Exam modes"
    q: "Can I stop and continue later?"
    a: "Yes. **Resume** takes you back to exactly where you stopped, on any device you sign in on."
  - group: "Explain, Quiz and Exam modes"
    q: "What is Quiz Mode?"
    a: "Low-pressure practice. You choose the subtopics and question types (multiple choice, true/false, open-ended and fill in the blank), answer, and get feedback straight away. You can ask why an answer is correct or incorrect. [Quiz Mode](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/quiz-mode/)."
  - group: "Explain, Quiz and Exam modes"
    q: "Why is there an \"I don't know\" button?"
    a: "Guessing hides what you don't know. Choosing **I don't know** records an honest answer, shows you the right one and gives a more accurate picture of your mastery. Your results show correct, incorrect and \"I don't know\" answers separately."
  - group: "Explain, Quiz and Exam modes"
    q: "What is Exam Mode?"
    a: "A formal assessment on a topic, a whole course or a learning plan. You choose the subtopics, question types, difficulty (Medium, Hard or a mix), the number of questions and whether it's timed. Answers are only marked when you submit. [Exam Mode](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/exam-mode/)."
  - group: "Explain, Quiz and Exam modes"
    q: "What do I see after an exam?"
    a: "Your score, a pass or fail at 70%, a full question review with feedback, an AI summary of your strengths and the areas to revisit, and your mastery level."
  - group: "Explain, Quiz and Exam modes"
    q: "How are open-ended answers marked?"
    a: "The AI reads what you actually wrote and marks it for accuracy and completeness, in the topic's language. You see the feedback in the question review."
  - group: "Listening, podcasts and speaking"
    q: "Can LearningClarity read the lesson to me?"
    a: "Yes. **Listen** reads the current Explain Mode section aloud, which helps auditory learners and lets you study away from the screen. Replaying a section you've already listened to is free. [Read it aloud](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/listen/)."
  - group: "Listening, podcasts and speaking"
    q: "Can I turn a topic into a podcast?"
    a: "Yes. Any topic or subtopic can become an AI podcast episode: a two-voice conversation between a host and an expert guest about your material. [Turn any topic into a podcast](/en/2026/10/turn-any-topic-into-a-podcast/)."
  - group: "Listening, podcasts and speaking"
    q: "How long are podcast episodes, and how long do they take?"
    a: "You choose from preset lengths: up to 10 minutes for a subtopic and up to 30 minutes for a whole topic. Episodes are made in the background, and an \"AI job complete\" notification tells you when one is ready. [Podcast episodes](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/podcast/)."
  - group: "Listening, podcasts and speaking"
    q: "Can I download podcast episodes?"
    a: "Yes, as MP3 files. The player also has speed control and keeps playing while you move around the app."
  - group: "Listening, podcasts and speaking"
    q: "Which languages do podcasts use?"
    a: "Episodes follow the topic's language. Voices are optimised for English, so other languages may carry a slight accent; Arabic topics use Arabic voices."
  - group: "Listening, podcasts and speaking"
    q: "What is Talk to This?"
    a: "A live voice tutor for the material you're studying. You speak or type, the tutor always answers aloud, and you can keep a transcript. It works on every platform: the web app, iPhone and Android. [Studying out loud](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/out-loud/)."
  - group: "Listening, podcasts and speaking"
    q: "How is Talk to This charged?"
    a: "By conversation length. Credits are reserved when a call starts and any unused reserved credits are returned when it ends, so you only pay for the time you use."
  - group: "Courses, learning plans and sharing"
    q: "What is an AI Course?"
    a: "A structured sequence of related topics. Describe what you want to learn and LearningClarity generates the course and its topics, and can suggest the best order to study them. [AI course creation](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/create-ai-course/)."
  - group: "Courses, learning plans and sharing"
    q: "What is an AI Learning Plan?"
    a: "A full curriculum of several courses towards a bigger goal, such as \"Prepare for a career in data analysis\". The structure is Learning Plan > Course > Topic > Subtopic. [Learning plans](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/learning-plan/)."
  - group: "Courses, learning plans and sharing"
    q: "Can I group topics I already created into a course?"
    a: "Yes. Create a course, add your existing topics and drag them into order, or let the AI suggest a prerequisites-first order. A topic belongs to one course at a time. [Courses and learning paths](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/course-management/)."
  - group: "Courses, learning plans and sharing"
    q: "Can I take one exam across a whole course or plan?"
    a: "Yes. A course exam covers its topics and a plan exam covers its courses, with the same settings as a topic exam. Results are saved in your exam history."
  - group: "Courses, learning plans and sharing"
    q: "Can I share a topic with a friend or colleague?"
    a: "Yes. Share any topic, course or plan you created with another LearningClarity user by email. They preview it and accept or decline. On accepting, they get their own independent copy with their own progress. [Sharing content](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/sharing-content/)."
  - group: "Courses, learning plans and sharing"
    q: "Can shared content be shared again?"
    a: "No. Only content you created yourself can be shared, and shared copies can't be shared onwards. You're notified when a share is accepted and can cancel pending shares."
  - group: "Progress, certificates and achievements"
    q: "How do I track my progress?"
    a: "Progress is tracked at every level: subtopics, topics, courses and plans, plus your quiz and exam history and mastery levels. Your dashboard shows a progress bar for each topic. [Tracking your progress](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/progress/)."
  - group: "Progress, certificates and achievements"
    q: "Where can I see my past exams?"
    a: "In **History**, on the Exams tab. Every attempt is saved separately, with your answers, the correct answers, explanations and AI recommendations for what to revisit. [Examination history](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/examination-history/)."
  - group: "Progress, certificates and achievements"
    q: "How do I earn a certificate?"
    a: "Score 70% or higher in one exam that covers every subtopic of a topic and has at least 30 questions. Finishing every subtopic of every topic in a course, or every course in a learning plan, also earns a certificate. [Certificates](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/certificates/)."
  - group: "Progress, certificates and achievements"
    q: "Do certificates cost anything?"
    a: "No. Earning, viewing, downloading and verifying certificates is free, and they never expire."
  - group: "Progress, certificates and achievements"
    q: "How can someone verify my certificate?"
    a: "Each certificate has a credential ID and a QR code. Anyone can open the verification link without an account and see that it's valid, what it was awarded for, who received it and when. Your score isn't shown there."
  - group: "Progress, certificates and achievements"
    q: "Is a LearningClarity certificate an accredited qualification?"
    a: "No. It proves you passed a real assessment, but it isn't accredited by a university or professional body and carries no academic credit. On LinkedIn, add it under Licenses & certifications with the credential ID and verification link. [What your certificate proves](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/certificate-value/)."
  - group: "Progress, certificates and achievements"
    q: "What are streaks and achievements?"
    a: "Your streak counts consecutive days with learning activity, and your longest streak records your best. There are 16 achievements with rarity levels from Common to Legendary, six of them hidden, such as First Topic, Quiz Perfect, Exam Excellence and 30-Day Streak. [Streaks and achievements](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/streaks-achievements/)."
  - group: "Credits and payments"
    q: "How much does LearningClarity cost?"
    a: "There's no subscription for individuals. You pay only for what you use with credits, and credit packs start at $1.99 for 1,000 credits. Credits never expire. [Credits and payments](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/credits/)."
  - group: "Credits and payments"
    q: "Which credit packs are available?"
    a: "Mini Starter: 1,000 credits for $1.99. Starter: 4,500 credits for $7.99. Advanced: 21,500 credits for $29.99. Ultimate: 45,000 credits for $59.99. The larger packs work out cheaper per credit."
  - group: "Credits and payments"
    q: "What uses credits?"
    a: "Generating new material: building topics, explanations, quizzes and exams, Listen, Visualize, podcasts and Talk to This. More complex work costs more, and podcast episodes show an estimate before you start. Reading, reviewing and verifying things you already have is free."
  - group: "Credits and payments"
    q: "Why does uploading need more credits than describing?"
    a: "Building a topic from your own files is much more work for the AI. Describing a topic starts from about 50 credits, while building one from uploads needs a balance of at least 1,000 credits."
  - group: "Credits and payments"
    q: "What happens when I run out of credits?"
    a: "Nothing is taken away. Your topics, progress, quiz and exam history and certificates all stay. A zero balance only stops new material from being generated until you top up."
  - group: "Credits and payments"
    q: "How do I pay, and is it safe?"
    a: "Buy credit packs by card on the web, or through the App Store on iPhone. Card payments are processed by Stripe, and LearningClarity never stores your card number."
  - group: "Credits and payments"
    q: "The checkout page won't load. What can I do?"
    a: "Antivirus browser extensions sometimes block the secure checkout page. Pause the extension, or try a private window, and reload. If checkout never loaded, nothing was charged. [Checkout troubleshooting](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/payment-troubleshooting/)."
  - group: "Credits and payments"
    q: "How does the referral program work?"
    a: "Every user has a referral code. A friend who signs up with it gets 200 bonus credits on top of the 300 welcome credits, 500 in total. When your friend makes their first credit purchase, you receive a bonus of 10% of the credits they buy, which can be converted to cash on request. A referral dashboard shows sign-ups, purchases and earnings."
  - group: "Languages, devices and accessibility"
    q: "Which languages does LearningClarity support?"
    a: "The app can be used in 7 languages: Arabic, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Spanish. Explanations, quizzes, exams, diagrams and podcasts are generated in your chosen learning language, not machine-translated. [Languages and accessibility](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/language-faq/)."
  - group: "Languages, devices and accessibility"
    q: "Can I upload material in one language and study it in another?"
    a: "Yes. The app language, the learning language of each topic and the language of your files are separate. Upload English slides and study them in German, for example, by choosing German when you create the topic. [Studying across languages](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/cross-language/)."
  - group: "Languages, devices and accessibility"
    q: "Does it support Arabic properly?"
    a: "Yes. The whole layout mirrors right-to-left, explanations use Modern Standard Arabic, and Listen and podcasts use Arabic voices."
  - group: "Languages, devices and accessibility"
    q: "Can I use LearningClarity to learn a foreign language?"
    a: "You can create a topic about a language with that language set as the learning language, and the spoken tutor will talk with you in it and correct one important mistake at a time. It isn't a full language course with levels or pronunciation scoring. [Learning a language](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/languages/)."
  - group: "Languages, devices and accessibility"
    q: "Does it work on my phone?"
    a: "Yes. There are apps for iPhone and Android, and the web version works in any up-to-date browser. Your library, progress and credits are shared across all your devices. [Devices, browsers and the app](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/devices/)."
  - group: "Languages, devices and accessibility"
    q: "Is LearningClarity suitable for children?"
    a: "Yes. Explanations, examples and questions are adapted to the learner's age, and parents, teachers or mentors can follow progress through Supervision, which only starts once both sides have agreed."
  - group: "Parents, teachers and supervision"
    q: "How does Supervision work?"
    a: "Supervision can start from either side: a parent, teacher or mentor sends a request to the learner's account email, or the learner invites them by email. Nothing is shared until the other person accepts. Supervisors then get scheduled email progress reports. [Supervision and parental tools](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/supervision/)."
  - group: "Parents, teachers and supervision"
    q: "What do supervisors see, and how often?"
    a: "Reports cover highlights, topic, course and plan progress, and recent quizzes and exams. They can be sent weekly, every two weeks or monthly at a chosen day and time, or immediately, in the supervisor's app language. Supervisors are also notified when the learner earns a certificate or has been inactive."
  - group: "Parents, teachers and supervision"
    q: "Can a supervisor change my work?"
    a: "No. Supervision is read-only: supervisors can't edit topics, take quizzes for you or see your sign-in details, and access can be ended at any time."
  - group: "Privacy, accuracy and your account"
    q: "Is my data safe?"
    a: "Each account can only access its own data, all traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are stored hashed, and Google, Microsoft and Apple sign-ins never share your password with LearningClarity. [Security and trust](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/security/)."
  - group: "Privacy, accuracy and your account"
    q: "What happens to the files I upload?"
    a: "You keep ownership of your files and of what is generated from them. They're used to build your topics, quizzes and exams, other learners can't see them, and they're never sold. To process them, your material is sent to a third-party AI service, so don't upload confidential or other people's personal data. [What happens to your uploads](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/privacy-uploads/)."
  - group: "Privacy, accuracy and your account"
    q: "Can the AI get things wrong?"
    a: "Yes, any AI can. Check anything surprising against your own material and use **Explain Further** to see whether a claim holds up. Studying from your own documents keeps explanations and questions anchored to the source you'll be examined on. [Accuracy and honest study](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/ai-accuracy/)."
  - group: "Privacy, accuracy and your account"
    q: "Is studying with AI cheating?"
    a: "Using it to understand and practise your material is studying. LearningClarity explains, questions, tests and grades you; it doesn't write essays for you to hand in. Rules on submitted work are set by your school, university or employer."
  - group: "Privacy, accuracy and your account"
    q: "How do I change my name, password or app language?"
    a: "Open your profile from the avatar at the top right. There you can update your details, change your password and switch the app language, which applies straight away. [Profile settings](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/profile/)."
  - group: "Privacy, accuracy and your account"
    q: "How do I delete my account?"
    a: "In the mobile app: open your profile, go to Account Actions, choose Delete Account and confirm by typing DELETE. Deletion is immediate and permanent and removes your content, unused credits and certificates, so download any certificate PDFs first. [Delete your account](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/delete-account/)."
