faq:
  - q: "What is LearningClarity?"
    a: "LearningClarity is an AI study app for the web, iPhone and Android. It turns your own notes, PDFs and documents, or any topic you describe, into step-by-step lessons, quizzes, timed exams and podcasts, in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Arabic."
  - q: "How do I create my first learning topic?"
    a: "There are two ways. **Describe a topic** in your own words, or **upload your material** (documents or images). Either way, LearningClarity builds the topic and its subtopics for you. [Read the guide](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/create-topic/)."
  - q: "What files can I upload?"
    a: "PDF (up to 40 MB per file), images in PNG, JPG or JPEG (up to 10 MB each, at most 7 per upload) and plain text .txt files (up to 5 MB), with up to 50 MB in one upload. Word documents and PowerPoint decks can't be uploaded directly: export them to PDF first and they upload normally. Audio and video files, including lecture recordings, aren't accepted. [Studying from your own notes](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/own-notes/)."
  - q: "How much does LearningClarity cost?"
    a: "LearningClarity uses a credit-based model, so you only pay for what you use. You receive free credits when you sign up, and additional credit packages start at $1.99 for 1,000 credits. There is no subscription, nothing renews, and credits never expire. [See all pricing tiers](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/credits/)."
  - q: "How many free credits do I get, and do I need a card?"
    a: "New accounts get 300 free credits the moment they are created, and 200 more if you sign up with another user's referral code. No card is asked for, there is no trial period, and nothing is charged automatically."
  - q: "Is there a referral program?"
    a: "Yes. Every user has a referral code. A friend who signs up with it gets 200 bonus credits on top of the 300 welcome credits, 500 in total. When your friend buys credits, you receive a bonus of 10% of the credits they purchased, and you can ask for that bonus to be converted to cash. [Credits and referrals](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/credits/)."
  - q: "What happens if I run out of credits?"
    a: "Nothing is taken away. Your topics, explanations, quiz and exam history, certificates and progress all stay where they are. A zero balance only stops new material from being generated."
  - q: "What languages are supported?"
    a: "LearningClarity is multilingual in three ways: the interface supports 7 languages (switchable from your Profile), you choose your explanation and quiz language when you create a topic, and uploaded material can be in any language, even a different one from the language you study in. [Languages and accessibility](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/language-faq/)."
  - q: "Is my data safe?"
    a: "Yes. Each account can only access its own data (Row-Level Security), all traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are stored hashed, and payments are handled by Stripe. [Security overview](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/security/)."
  - q: "Can I turn my topic into a podcast?"
    a: "Yes. Any topic or subtopic can become an audio episode: a host interviewing an expert guest, in the language your content is written in. Most episodes are ready in about 30 to 60 seconds, and you can download them as MP3."
  - q: "How do I earn a certificate?"
    a: "Pass an exam with 70% or higher that covers every subtopic in the topic and has at least 30 questions, or finish every topic in a course, or every course in a learning plan. Certificates are free, and anyone can verify one without signing in. They are verifiable, but they are not an accredited qualification. [Certificates](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/certificates/)."
  - q: "Does it work on my phone?"
    a: "Yes. The web version runs in a phone browser with the same features as on a laptop, and there are also apps for iPhone and Android. A laptop is simply more comfortable for large uploads and long timed exams."
  - q: "Can the AI get things wrong?"
    a: "It can. Any AI can produce a confident sentence that is wrong. Check anything surprising against your own material, and use **Explain Further** to see whether a claim still holds up when it is expanded. Studying from your own uploaded documents keeps explanations, questions and exams anchored to the source you will be examined on. [Accuracy and honest study](https://www.learningclarity.ai/help/ai-accuracy/)."
  - q: "Is studying with AI cheating?"
    a: "Using it to understand your material is studying. Submitting generated text as your own work is a different thing, and that rule is set by your school, university or employer. LearningClarity explains, questions, tests and grades you; it doesn't write essays for you to hand in."
