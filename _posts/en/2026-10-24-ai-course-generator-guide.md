Some goals are too big for a single topic. "Understand personal finance" or "get ready for a role in project management" covers several subjects that build on each other. An **AI course generator** helps here: you describe the goal once, and LearningClarity turns it into a structured course of related topics, ready to study in a sensible order.

## How the AI course generator works

{% include scene.html n=1 %}

An **AI Course** in LearningClarity groups several related topics into a structured sequence. Creating one takes three steps:

1. **Describe what you want to learn.** Write your goal in plain language, the way you would explain it to a tutor.
2. **Let LearningClarity build the course.** It generates the course and its topics for you, and each topic comes with its own subtopics.
3. **Follow the suggested order.** LearningClarity can suggest the best order in which to study the topics, so you always know what comes next.

If you only need one subject, [describing a single topic](/en/2026/10/learn-any-subject-with-ai/) is the quickest way to start. A course is the better choice when the goal spans several subjects.

## Three situations where a structured course helps

{% include scene.html n=2 %}

**Preparing for a new job.** A new role often means learning several things at once: the tools, the processes and the vocabulary of the field. A course keeps them together as one structured learning path instead of a pile of separate notes.

**Supporting a child through a school subject.** A parent can create a course for a subject a child is studying, with the topics in a suggested order. Explanations and questions are pitched at the learner's age.

{% include scene.html n=3 %}

**Training a team.** Training leads can build a course once and share it by email with colleagues who use LearningClarity. Each person who accepts gets an independent copy and tracks their own progress.

{% include scene.html n=4 %}

## From the first topic to a certificate

Inside the course, every topic works like any other topic in LearningClarity. Open a subtopic in [Explain Mode, the step-by-step AI tutor](/en/2026/10/meet-your-step-by-step-ai-tutor/), which teaches one short section at a time. Practise with **Quiz Mode** when you want low-pressure feedback.

When you are ready, **Exam Mode** can assess a single topic or the whole course, with the scope, question types, difficulty and timing you choose. Qualifying exam results earn a certificate for the topic or the course. Progress is tracked at every level, so you can see how far through the course you are.
