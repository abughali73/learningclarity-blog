Changing careers is exciting, but the first question is often the hardest: where do I even start? An **AI learning plan** answers it. You describe the bigger goal, for example "Prepare for a career in data analysis", and LearningClarity turns it into a full curriculum of courses you can follow step by step.

## What an AI learning plan gives you

An **AI Learning Plan** in LearningClarity is a complete curriculum made of several courses, all pointing toward one broader goal. LearningClarity generates the plan and its courses for you, and it can suggest the best order in which to study them.

Everything sits in a clear structure: **Learning Plan > Course > Topic > Subtopic**. The plan holds the big picture, each course covers one area, each topic focuses on one subject, and the subtopics are the bite-sized pieces you actually study. If you have used an [AI course generator to build a single course](/en/2026/10/ai-course-generator-guide/), a plan is the next step up: several courses, working together.

## A career change, step by step

Imagine you work in retail and want to move into data analysis. Here is how a plan keeps the move manageable:

1. **Describe the goal.** Write it in plain words, the way you would explain it to a mentor.
2. **Review the curriculum.** LearningClarity generates the courses that make up the plan, each with its own topics and subtopics.
3. **Follow the suggested order.** Start with the course LearningClarity suggests first, so the basics come before the harder material.
4. **Focus where you need it.** Inside each topic you choose which subtopics to focus on, and completion is tracked for each one.

Your plan can also be in the language you want to learn in. New plans default to your app language, and you can change the learning language before you create one.

## Learn, practise and prove it

Each topic in the plan works like any other topic in LearningClarity. **Explain Mode** teaches one short section at a time, **Quiz Mode** gives you low-pressure practice, and **Exam Mode** can assess a single topic or a whole course. Qualifying exam results earn a certificate for the topic or course, which is a useful milestone on a career change.

Progress is tracked at every level, from subtopics up to courses and the plan itself, so on a busy week you can open the app and see exactly how far you have come.

## Plans for teams and families too

The same idea works beyond a personal career move. A training lead can build a plan once and share it by email with colleagues who use LearningClarity; each person who accepts gets an independent copy and their own progress. A parent or mentor who supervises a learner receives email reports that include plan progress.
