Today we are introducing an **AI study platform subscription** for LearningClarity. Many of you told us you study a little almost every day, and you would rather have one small, predictable monthly price than keep an eye on credit balances.

We listened. From now on, the LearningClarity study platform works with a free first week, simple monthly plans, and a usage meter you can always see. To show how it fits into a real routine, we'll follow Ingrid, a fictional physiotherapist preparing for a sports-rehabilitation certificate alongside a full-time job.

## Day 1: a free week to find your rhythm

{% include scene.html n=1 %}

Ingrid signs up on a Sunday evening with her Google account. Her exam is six weeks away, and she has no idea yet how much she'll actually study on weeknights.

That is exactly what the free week is for. **Every new account gets one week of free use with a fair usage allowance.** Ingrid describes her topic, opens the first subtopic in Explain Mode, and tries a Quiz before bed. By Friday she knows her pattern: about forty minutes a day, plus a longer session at the weekend.

## Week 2: a small plan for everyday study

{% include scene.html n=2 %}

When the free week ends, Ingrid picks **Bronze for $2.99 a month**. It is built for exactly her kind of steady, everyday learning, with a fair usage allowance that lets her keep going without thinking about it.

On the tram she listens to a podcast of the subtopic she read the night before. Nothing about her studying changes. What changes is the bill: one small, predictable amount.

## A usage meter that does the planning for you

{% include scene.html n=3 %}

Two things make the subscription easy to manage.

- **You always see the percentage of your allowance used.** The platform shows it at all times, so you can plan ahead instead of being surprised.
- **Every feature is clearly labelled when it uses your allowance.** Before you tap, you know what counts.

Three weeks before her exam, Ingrid checks the meter on a Sunday night: 70% used, and her heaviest week still to come. She has two simple choices.

## When you need more: top up or upgrade

**Buy a top-up.** For **$3.99**, a top-up adds one Bronze-sized allowance. You can buy several at once, and each purchase is charged once, not every month. Ingrid buys one for her exam week.

**Move to a larger plan.** If more intensive study becomes your normal, Silver and Gold give you far more room each month and work out cheaper per unit of usage. Silver costs about $2.40 per Bronze-sized allowance, Gold about $1.60.

## The AI study platform subscription plans at a glance

| Plan | Monthly allowance | Monthly price | Yearly price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free week | Fair usage, first 7 days | Free | n/a |
| Bronze | 1× (base allowance) | $2.99 | $32.99 |
| Silver | 5× Bronze | $11.99 | $139.99 |
| Gold | 20× Bronze | $31.99 | $370.99 |
| Top-up | +1× Bronze, one-off | $3.99 each | n/a |

For every plan, paying yearly costs less than twelve monthly payments.

Ingrid finishes her exam week without once wondering whether she can afford another practice quiz. That is the point of the new plans: you focus on learning, and the cost stays simple.
