Waiting for a lesson to finish writing is a small thing that breaks focus. As of today, it no longer happens in LearningClarity.

Our 8 November feature update rebuilds the lesson itself in the web app:

• Lessons start straight away: page 1 appears while the rest is written, with an outline of every planned page.
• Redesigned lesson tools: each tool says what it works on and what it does, for selected text or the whole page.
• Lessons planned for your level: choose from "New to me" to "I know it well" when you create a topic, an AI Course or an AI Learning Plan.
• Knowledge checks you can retry, then a wrap-up that suggests your next step.
• Revise what you missed after a quiz or exam, one weak area at a time or all of them in a row.
• Play with this chart: change any chart in a lesson yourself, free and with no AI.
• Change your sign-in email from your profile.

To show it in use, we follow Lina, a fictional first-year student working through "Photosynthesis basics", with short screen clips of every feature.

All seven are in the web app for now; the iPhone and Android apps follow later.
