Six weeks. A 300-page PDF. Two slide decks. A 40-minute commute each way.

Beatriz, a procurement coordinator, asked her study group which study app to use for her certification exam. She got four different answers: Quizlet, Anki, StudyFetch and Knowt.

Our new blog post follows her six weeks and compares all five tools fairly:

• Knowt: free flashcards with Learn mode and spaced repetition
• StudyFetch: an AI tutor that answers from your uploads
• Anki: long-term memorisation with the FSRS scheduler
• Quizlet: ready-made sets for popular courses
• LearningClarity: learn your own material in order in Explain Mode, then rehearse with Quiz Mode and timed exams in Exam Mode

Her setback came in week four, when a practice exam showed exactly which subtopics to fix. Her payoff: she walked into the exam having studied every subtopic in order, without writing a single flashcard.
