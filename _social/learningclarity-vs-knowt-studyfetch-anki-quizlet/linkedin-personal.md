"Which study app should I use?" is one of the questions I hear most from learners preparing for an exam.

So this week on the LearningClarity blog I answered it with a story. Beatriz has six weeks until a certification exam, a 300-page PDF and a 40-minute commute, and each of her classmates swears by a different app.

My honest take after comparing them:

• If you need to keep thousands of facts for months, Anki is hard to beat, as long as you are willing to build the cards.
• If a set for your course already exists, Quizlet saves you time.
• If you want free flashcards, look at Knowt. If you learn by asking questions, StudyFetch is built for that.
• If you need to understand a whole syllabus in order and rehearse the real exam, that is what we built LearningClarity for: Explain Mode, then Quiz Mode, then timed exams in Exam Mode, with podcasts and a live voice tutor for the commute.

Many learners combine tools, and that is fine. Start from what your exam actually tests.
